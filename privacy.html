Privacy

Perks Ledger collects nothing.

  • No accounts. There is no signup, login, or user database.
  • No server. The app is a static page; your cards, check-offs, and subscriptions live in your browser’s localStorage on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • No analytics or trackers. The production build makes zero third-party requests.
  • Backups are yours. Export/Import and “Download app + data” produce files on your device that only you hold.
  • Email is opt-in. Feedback / wrong-data / card-request flows open your mail app or copy a report you control; nothing is sent unless you send it. Emails go to mybbrllc@gmail.com and are used only to fix data and improve the app.
  • Caveat: because data is browser-local, clearing site data (or a browser deciding to purge unused sites) deletes your wallet — the app reminds you to keep a backup.
