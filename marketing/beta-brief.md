# Perks Ledger — beta tester brief

Send this (or paste it into the group chat) with the link.

---

Hey! I built an app that tracks every credit-card perk so you stop leaving
money on the table. It's free, no signup, and your data never leaves your
phone. I'd love your honest feedback. 5-minute setup:

**https://perksledger.vercel.app**

1. **Open it on your phone**, then make it an app: iPhone → Share → *Add to
   Home Screen* · Android → menu → *Install app*. (The app will remind you.)
2. **Add your cards** — or tap *Explore a sample wallet* first to see how it
   works. Phone plans and memberships (Verizon, T-Mobile, Prime, Walmart+,
   Costco) count too.
3. **Tap "What do you pay for?"** in the Perk connections box and tick your
   subscriptions — this is the magic part: it finds things you're paying for
   that your cards already cover.
4. **Export a backup once** (Data → Export). Your data lives only in your
   browser — if you never open the site for weeks, iPhones can clear it.
5. **Check perks off as you use them.** Come back when the app nags you about
   credits that are about to reset.
6. **Tell me what's wrong** — every perk has a "Report wrong data" link, and
   there's a feedback link in the footer. Wrong numbers are the #1 thing I
   want to hear about.

Known limits (so you don't have to report them): US cards only for now, no
multi-device sync yet (use Export/Import to move data), and benefit values
change whenever banks feel like it — the footer shows when I last verified
everything.
