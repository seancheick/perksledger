# Privacy

Perks Ledger collects nothing.

- **No accounts.** There is no signup, login, or user database.
- **No server.** The app is a static page; your cards, check-offs, and
  subscriptions live in your browser's `localStorage` on your device and are
  never transmitted anywhere.
- **No analytics or trackers.** The production build makes zero third-party
  requests.
- **Backups are yours.** Export/Import and "Download app + data" produce
  files on your device that only you hold.
- **Email is opt-in.** The feedback / wrong-data / card-request links open
  your own mail app addressed to mybbrllc@gmail.com; nothing is sent unless
  you hit send. Emails are used only to fix data and improve the app.
- **Caveat:** because data is browser-local, clearing site data (or a browser
  deciding to purge unused sites) deletes your wallet — the app reminds you
  to keep a backup.
